Website Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

This policy covers the website digreno.com, operated by DigReno (“DIGRENO”, “we”). Each DIGRENO product has its own privacy policy:

What this website collects

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect personal data when you browse it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

When you email us

If you email us, we keep your message and contact details to reply and to follow up on your request. We do not sell them or add you to marketing lists without asking. Ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the date above.
