How it works

Four steps from order to PDF

Set up your business once. After that, every invoice follows the same short path.

  1. Details

    Invoice number and date fill in automatically. Pick a customer or add a new one with their GSTIN.

  2. Items

    Add goods or services from your product list, with HSN/SAC, unit, rate and GST slab.

  3. Summary

    Discounts, CGST, SGST or IGST, round-off and the total in words, worked out for you.

  4. Preview

    Check the finished invoice, then generate the PDF to print or send on WhatsApp.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Invoices, customers and products are stored on your phone and work offline. You need internet to log in, buy credits and sync your credit balance.

When is CGST + SGST applied and when IGST?

If the place of supply is in the same state as your business, CGST and SGST are applied. If it is a different state, IGST is applied. The app decides this from the place of supply you select.

Can I edit an invoice after generating it?

Yes. Open the invoice and tap Edit. Updating an invoice you already generated does not use another credit.

What is HSN / SAC?

HSN codes classify goods and SAC codes classify services. Enter 4, 6 or 8 digits as your turnover requires. Service codes usually start with 99.

Can I print on a thermal printer?

Yes. The Thermal template is laid out for 80 mm receipt printers. A4 and A5 templates print on any regular printer.

Do credits expire?

No. Purchased credits do not expire. Every pack is a one-time purchase and nothing renews automatically.

I changed my phone. Are my credits lost?

No. Your credit balance is kept on our server. Log in with the same mobile number and your balance comes back automatically.

How do I keep my invoices safe?

Your invoices are stored on your phone. Keep Android backup turned on for your Google account so app data is restored on a new phone. You can also export a backup from Settings.

Money was deducted but credits were not added.

Credits usually appear within a few minutes once the bank confirms. In the app, open Help → Payment issues and tap Check status. Purchases are final, but you always get the credits you paid for. See our refund policy.

Is DIGRENO Invoice available on iPhone?

Not yet. The app is launching on Android first.
