Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

DIGRENO Invoice sells invoice credits as one-time packs. There are no subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews automatically.

Payment taken but credits not added

Credits are usually added within a few minutes after your bank confirms the payment. If they don’t appear:

  1. In the app, open Help → Payment issues and tap Check status on the payment.
  2. If it still shows as pending, wait up to 24 hours. Banks sometimes confirm late.
  3. If credits still haven’t been added, raise a support ticket with the Razorpay payment ID (starts with pay_).

If we cannot add the credits, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice for the same order, or charged for a payment that failed, we refund the extra amount in full.

Unused credit packs

You can request a refund for a pack within 7 days of purchase if none of the credits from that pack have been used. Write to us with the payment ID. Once any credit from a pack has been used, that pack cannot be refunded.

Not refundable

  • Credits already used to generate invoices.
  • Free trial and promotional bonus credits.
  • Credits remaining on an account that you deleted or that we suspended for misuse.

Refund timelines

Approved refunds are started within 2 business days. The money reaches you in 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. UPI refunds are often faster.

Contact

Email support@digrenoinvoice.com with your registered mobile number and payment ID.
